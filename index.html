01The Operator

Multiple Lives,
One Engine

From DJ booths to dealership floors, to print awards, to AI deployments. Every chapter built the same skill: seeing the system nobody else sees, then building the machine that fixes it.

I have lived multiple lives. Creative professional. DJ. Agency founder. Marketing director. Husband. Father. Mental health patient. Survivor. Partner. Corporate operator. AI builder. Future founder.

I started in vocational schools, running Facebook ads for trade programs. That first break came from Alex Andreus, a dealership group owner who saw something in me. One school became three. Two stayed for seven-plus years. One is still active today.

That became Beyond Creative, my digital marketing agency. I grew it from zero to $10K a month in recurring retainers, then to $600K a year over five years. Nearly fifteen employees. Around twenty clients. Average tenure three plus years, some seven plus. I specialized in powersports dealers, generating website leads, fixing lead nurturing pipelines, and building BDC processes that actually converted.

Then I walked through the worst depression of my life. I came out the other side with a systems approach to my own mind, not just my business. Now I am building Boostcon, named after the Boost Blue Pearl on my Type R, because the color of the car I love became the metaphor for everything I am building next.

07Operating Philosophy

How I
Operate

I did not build these in a classroom. I built them in crisis, in recovery, and in the quiet hours after everything almost fell apart.

01

The Three Zone Protector Rule

Red, Yellow, and Green zones govern my energy and decision making. I do not operate in Red. I do not make major decisions in Yellow. I build systems in Green and deploy them when the storm hits.

"Preserve the judgment of calm Joe for use by overwhelmed Joe."
02

Document Behavior, Not Motive

I do not prosecute motive. I document what happened, when it happened, and what it cost. This applies to business relationships, co parenting, and my own internal narrative.

"No major decision while activated unless immediate danger."
03

The Life Command Center

I build systems to prevent valuable things from being lost. Revenue, time, potential, context. My Life Command Center runs on Next.js and Supabase. Ten of twelve sections are live.

"I build systems so intelligence and fatherhood stop competing for the same exhausted hour."
04

The 72 Hour Pause

No emotional purchases. No new recurring expense based on expected money. When I feel the urge to buy, fix, or commit from a place of anxiety, I wait 72 hours. Most decisions dissolve. The ones that survive are real.

"Urgency is often emotional, not operational."
05

One Correction, One Escalation, One Decision

In work and in life, I do not stack interventions. One correction at a time. One escalation at a time. One decision at a time. The system handles the rest. This is how I prevent overwhelm from becoming paralysis.

"What actually moves the needle?"
06

Stoicism as Operating System

I did not read about Stoicism in a book club. I found it in the hardest year of my life, when everything I was carrying finally broke. It became the framework that let me separate facts from emotional interpretation and keep moving.

"It takes time, but do not waste time."

Father of Four

JJ, Kyle, Ale, and Lily. Everything I build is built so I can be present for them.

Being a father of four across two families has taught me more about systems, priorities, and emotional regulation than any business role. The hardest leadership challenge is not managing a team. It is being present, consistent, and grounded for four people who need different things from you at the same time.

One of my proudest moments as a dad came when my son noticed a kid being left out at practice and befriended him. He recognized the kid's struggle because he had been there himself. My approach to parenting is structured: fatherly love first, rigid expectations second. No shaming. Clear boundaries. Consistent consequences. The same framework I use in business: document behavior, do not prosecute motive.
